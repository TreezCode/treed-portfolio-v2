A useful first story

Internal editorial plan. This is a starting point for choosing an edit, not publication-ready campaign copy.

  1. Open with the change. Put the two homepage captures together and explain why the portfolio needed to reflect more of your personality.
  2. Show the design decision. Introduce Living Archive through its geometric identity, full-image framing and selected work.
  3. Show the structure. Compare the old Work section with the new Work page and deeper case studies.
  4. Make the creativity tangible. Show Geometry Bench and one shape or palette change.
  5. Offer a small world to enter. Show Portal Keeper's light puzzle and the case study that explains it.
  6. End with what comes next. Invite specific playtest feedback once a reachable preview is ready. Explain that player feedback will inform the next room.

Reuse the same material

Start with a six-slide before-and-after carousel. Follow with a short Bench or Portal Keeper clip that shows one clear interaction. A longer build article can then connect the design choices, procedural geometry, shared puzzle model, fallbacks and what the playtest teaches you.

Use observed changes and implementation facts. Keep “local candidate,” “published,” and “playtested” distinct as the project moves forward. Choose the final platform, captions and publication date after reviewing the captures.
