## Product principles

1. **A clear first impression, meaningful depth.** The strongest work earns attention; individual project stories and optional experiments reward exploration.
2. **Personality belongs in the work.** Original worlds, practical products and specific details express Joey's taste.
3. **Evidence supports confidence.** State supported capabilities plainly. Never invent independent authorship, measured results, production readiness or client acceptance.
4. **Motion explains relationships.** Titles, actions and content remain readable while imagery supplies movement and dimension.
5. **Security is part of the craft.** Privacy, authorization, validation and failure behavior matter where the project calls for them.
6. **Visitors stay in control.** Native scroll, keyboard navigation, touch and reduced motion need no special instructions.
7. **Employment leads; business services have a home.** Keep the main journey professional and route service buyers to Codorus Web.

## Accessibility and resilience

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, with semantic landmarks, useful labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, usable touch targets and responsive text. Essential identity, content and actions survive unavailable JavaScript, motion, WebGL or optional media. Give ongoing motion a clear pause control and respect reduced motion.

Record actual verification coverage rather than treating browser emulation as physical-device acceptance. Normal-motion composition and behavior require review alongside reduced motion.
