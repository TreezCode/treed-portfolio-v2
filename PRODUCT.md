## Product purpose

Build With Treez will market founder-led custom website rebuilds for established local-service businesses. Roofing is the first evidence vertical because it is the only trade in which Build With Treez currently has meaningful client-project experience.

The website should help a matched owner understand the offer, inspect honest evidence of craft, meet the person accountable for the work, and request a manual website review or project conversation. It is not expected to create demand on its own; direct outreach, referrals, and useful founder-led content must bring the right people to it.

“Premium” and “award-caliber” are internal quality standards. Until awards or measured commercial outcomes exist, they are not public proof claims.

## Initial customer

The first outbound audience is an owner-led roofing company that:

- already has a credible operation, real projects, reviews, and the capacity to accept good work;
- receives some demand through referrals, its Google Business Profile, advertising, networking, or existing search visibility;
- has a dated, generic, confusing, insecure, slow, or difficult-to-maintain website;
- values direct accountability and a custom result rather than the cheapest possible brochure site; and
- can provide accurate service facts, project evidence, customer language, and timely decisions.

Build With Treez may still accept a strong-fit client from another local-service trade. It will not claim broad trade expertise or build speculative libraries for other niches before the expansion gate passes.

## Primary offer

The initial offer is a **Custom Service-Business Website Rebuild** with a roofing-first proof set.

Depending on scope, the engagement may include:

- business diagnosis and content hierarchy;
- client-specific positioning, copy direction, and art direction;
- responsive implementation and an appropriate CMS;
- accessible interactions and reduced-motion behavior;
- technical search foundations, structured content, redirects, and measurement setup;
- qualified inquiry paths, form delivery, QA, and launch controls; and
- a defined post-launch defect period.

The controlled promise is a distinctive, credible, maintainable, and measurement-ready website that makes the business, its services, its proof, and the next action easier to understand. Build With Treez does **not** promise rankings, traffic, lead volume, revenue, or return on investment.

Founding scope hypothesis: **$5,000 to $8,000** for a tightly bounded five-to-eight-route marketing site with one straightforward CMS need. Larger content, brand, commerce, integration, photography, or ongoing marketing scopes are quoted separately. This range is an internal sales hypothesis, not a public price, a claim of market validation, or a minimum that must be reached before the model is considered credible.

Ongoing SEO, content operations, advertising, broad brand identity work, unlimited revisions, and guaranteed marketing outcomes are not part of the initial offer. Maintenance or content support may be scoped case by case after a real client need exists.

## Primary conversion

A qualified website-review or project-conversation request from a decision-maker whose business, problem, evidence, budget, and timing reasonably fit the offer.

## Proof model

Build With Treez will use three honest kinds of evidence:

1. **Client work** — real, permissioned work with its exact status and outcomes stated accurately.
2. **Concept work** — clearly fictional work that demonstrates design and implementation capability, never business results.
3. **Process and technical evidence** — decision artifacts, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, CMS, form, QA, and launch evidence that proves only what was actually tested.

Ascension Roofing is an active client rebuild, not a fictional concept and not yet an outcome case study. With Joshua’s permission, it may show the audit, strategy, art direction, Roof Record workflow, responsive implementation, and launch preparation under a conspicuous “rebuild in progress; production results are not yet available” disclosure.

## Industry Concepts

“Industry Concepts” is the internal name for a curated capability portfolio and sales-conversation aid. Publicly, the simpler labels **Work**, **Client Work**, **Concept Work**, and **Roofing** should be preferred.

The lean first release is:

- the Build With Treez marketing site itself;
- Ascension as separately labeled active client work, only with permission; and
- no more than one original fictional roofing concept before market feedback.

The fictional concept is a polished responsive vertical slice, not a speculative ten-page business. It must demonstrate a materially different roofing customer, brand thesis, proof approach, and visual language from Ascension. A second roofing concept is allowed only when the gate in `INDUSTRY_CONCEPTS_PLAN.md` shows that it would answer a real sales or portfolio need.

Each fictional concept must be unmistakably labeled. Any interactive demo is `noindex`, excluded from sitemaps, and free of fabricated reviews, companies, results, locations, credentials, and structured business evidence. Clients buy a new custom engagement at the demonstrated level of craft; they do not buy the displayed design or downloadable source.

No plumbing, lawn-care, or other niche concept may begin before the second-niche gate passes.

## Success measures

Primary commercial evidence:

- substantive conversations with matched roofing owners;
- qualified project conversations;
- proposals issued;
- paid deposits and engagements closed;
- delivery hours, direct costs, scope health, and client experience; and
- client-authorized capability or outcome case studies.

Traffic, impressions, social reach, awards, Lighthouse scores, and concept views are diagnostic evidence. They are not proof of revenue, lead quality, or return on investment.

## Durable delivery principles

1. Diagnose the business before choosing an aesthetic.
2. Produce exactly two materially different directions for a real client and stop for Treez’s selection.
3. Build one vertical slice before expanding a site or concept.
4. Repeat the process and technical primitives, never the client-facing identity.
5. Keep proof adjacent to claims and never invent outcomes, reviews, credentials, locations, or availability.
6. Treat mobile composition, accessibility, reduced motion, performance, failure behavior, and measurement as design quality.
7. Use award-caliber as an internal review standard; never claim an award that does not exist.
8. Keep sales operations outside the premium-client-site skill and the AIEOS kernel.
9. Add AIEOS governance only when integration, data, security, cost, or coordination risk justifies it.
10. Do not create a framework, CMS, animation dependency, model integration, or operating system without a demonstrated project need.

## IP and originality

Build With Treez retains pre-existing tools, workflows, generic components, schemas, and generalized know-how. Client agreements define ownership or exclusive license for paid client-specific deliverables after payment, subject to third-party licenses.

Client logos, copy, photographs, project data, testimonials, custom illustrations, proof-system expression, and signature visual treatment are not reusable inventory. Ascension’s Weather Shield, Roof Record expression, visual language, copy, evidence, and branded execution are protected from resale or silent reuse. Portfolio display requires permission.

## Non-goals

- A downloadable template store or cheap theme business
- Two or three full speculative sites in every niche
- Mass-cloned trade or city pages
- Fictional work presented as client work
- Traffic, ranking, conversion, lead, revenue, award, or ROI claims without direct evidence
- Care, growth, SEO, or content retainers before delivery capability and demand are proven
- A CRM, outreach engine, or social scheduler inside the website-production workflow
- Rebuilding AIEOS around a process already handled by the premium-client-site skill
- Carrying the current portfolio’s visual system into the rebuild without two new directions and human approval

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Require keyboard navigation, visible focus, strong contrast, meaningful landmarks and names, 44 CSS-pixel touch targets, reduced-motion parity, responsive art direction, and essential content that remains useful when WebGL, animation, JavaScript, or optional media fails.

## Stop conditions

Stop and request Treez’s decision when:

- a public statement implies an outcome that current evidence does not prove;
- a concept could be confused with a real company or client result;
- Ascension material lacks Joshua’s permission or accurate status labeling;
- a client-specific expression would be reused;
- a second roofing concept lacks the evidence required by its gate;
- another niche is proposed before the second-niche gate;
- recurring marketing services are proposed before delivery capability exists;
- a material creative direction lacks human approval;
- legal, licensing, accessibility, privacy, security, or advertising concerns remain unresolved; or
- the detailed plan conflicts with this file.

## Change control

- Durable decisions are: custom engagements rather than template sales, roofing-first evidence, truthful proof labels, protected client IP, controlled claims, proportional tooling, and evidence-gated expansion.
- Pricing, route names, concept format, outreach volume, and channel tests are hypotheses. Treez may revise them after recording the evidence and decision in `INDUSTRY_CONCEPTS_PLAN.md`.
- A template marketplace, indexed fictional business, weaker client-protection rule, unsubstantiated outcome promise, or mass niche expansion requires explicit evidence, impact analysis, Treez approval, and a major-version update.
