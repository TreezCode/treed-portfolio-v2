# Build With Treez Industry Concepts Plan

## Authority

- Status: **PROPOSED FOR TREEZ REVIEW**
- Version: **2.0**
- Decision owner: Treez
- Recorded: August 3, 2026
- Applies to: the future `buildwithtreez.com` marketing site, its work portfolio, and any fictional trade concepts used in sales
- Authority relationship: `PRODUCT.md` is the canonical business authority. This file contains the execution sequence, evidence rules, hypotheses, and gates.
- Current-site relationship: the deployed portfolio remains operational until the rebuild launches. `DESIGN.md` describes the legacy implementation only. A new client-site brief and approved design contract will govern the rebuild.

This plan deliberately distinguishes durable decisions from testable hypotheses. It should prevent drift without turning untested prices, page counts, timelines, or outreach quotas into false certainty.

## Strategic answer

The original library idea is directionally right. The useful version is a curated portfolio organized by industry, not a template marketplace and not a separate software product.

The long-term shape may eventually contain two or three examples in roofing, plumbing, lawn care, and other trades. The honest first release is smaller:

1. Build With Treez as proof of Treez’s own positioning, taste, and implementation quality.
2. Ascension Roofing as permissioned **active client work**, with its in-progress status and lack of production outcomes stated plainly.
3. One clearly fictional roofing concept showing a materially different business and creative thesis.

That is enough to begin a sales validation test. Additional fictional production is earned by prospect feedback, paid work, or a demonstrated portfolio gap. The website supports sales; direct conversations create the initial demand.

## Why this is the right comparison to Colorado Web Forge

Colorado Web Forge’s strongest trust signals are not a large speculative concept system. They are a visible founder, a clear custom service, inspectable live client work, named reviews, an understandable process, one measured search case, and a low-friction manual website audit.

Build With Treez should borrow that operating clarity, not its proof volume or stronger outcome language. Colorado Web Forge can currently point to numerous launched sites. Build With Treez must present its earlier-stage evidence with equal clarity:

- who is accountable for the work;
- what is custom and what is reusable infrastructure;
- what Ascension proves today;
- what a fictional concept can and cannot prove;
- what the client receives;
- how the project reduces risk through decisions and verification; and
- what simple next step begins a conversation.

The goal is not to look like an agency with years of results. It is to look like a capable, honest founder who can explain and execute a high-quality engagement.

## Evidence baseline

### What can be proved now

- Treez can audit and restructure a complex local-service website.
- Treez can develop distinct art direction and original supporting assets.
- Treez can build responsive, accessible, motion-aware interfaces.
- Treez can design intent-aware contact paths and an appropriate project-gallery workflow.
- Treez can implement technical search foundations, measurement setup, forms, CMS behavior, QA, and launch controls when those items are actually completed and documented.
- Ascension is a real client rebuild in progress, subject to Joshua’s permission for public display.

### What cannot be proved yet

- that Treez has increased a client’s qualified traffic, rankings, calls, leads, revenue, or conversion rate;
- that the Ascension rebuild has produced a commercial result before production launch and a meaningful measurement period;
- that Treez is a proven roofing, SEO, GEO, conversion, or growth expert;
- that the work is award-winning; or
- that any website will pay for itself.

Capability evidence is enough to test whether the design and implementation offer can sell. It must not be relabeled as outcome evidence.

## Claim contract

| Safe when accurate | Not safe until directly evidenced |
|---|---|
| “I design and build original, responsive websites for service businesses.” | “Proven websites that generate qualified leads.” |
| “Founder-led strategy, art direction, development, accessibility, technical search foundations, QA, and measurement setup.” | “SEO expert,” “growth partner,” or guaranteed visibility. |
| “Designed to make services, proof, and next steps easier to understand.” | “Conversion-optimized,” “sites that convert,” or a stated conversion lift without measured evidence. |
| “Fictional roofing concept demonstrating design and implementation capability.” | A concept presented as a launched business, case study, or client result. |
| “Ascension is an active rebuild; production results are not yet available.” | Traffic, lead, ranking, revenue, or ROI improvement attributed to the unlaunched rebuild. |
| “Performance-tested,” “measurement-ready,” or “WCAG 2.2 AA target” when supported by named checks. | “Award-winning,” “best,” “guaranteed,” or “pays for itself.” |

## Offer to validate

### Custom Service-Business Website Rebuild

One focused custom engagement is the initial business. It should include only the scope a client actually needs, which may cover:

- diagnosis, positioning, content hierarchy, and route planning;
- two materially different visual directions and one approved system;
- original copy direction, art direction, and signature media;
- approximately five to eight core marketing routes for the founding scope;
- one straightforward project, gallery, or content CMS where needed;
- intent-aware inquiry behavior and reliable form delivery;
- responsive, accessibility, performance, security, and failure-state QA;
- technical search foundations, redirects, analytics, and conversion events; and
- launch support plus a written defect period.

Explicit exclusions unless separately scoped:

- unlimited pages, revisions, or support;
- a full identity rebrand or photography production;
- ongoing SEO, Google Business Profile management, content publishing, advertising, or social media;
- complex integrations, ecommerce, portals, or applications; and
- rankings, traffic, lead, revenue, or ROI guarantees.

### Price hypothesis

Test a fixed proposal rather than publishing a rigid package before enough sales evidence exists.

- Initial internal range: **$5,000 to $8,000** for the founding scope above.
- Larger scope may exceed that range; reduced scope should reduce deliverables rather than quietly discounting the same workload.
- Record estimated and actual hours, direct costs, revisions, and support load.
- A paid deposit at a sustainable, clearly scoped price is commercial evidence. An arbitrary $8,000 minimum is not a qualification test.
- Never condition a concession on a positive review. Any case-study participation or honest feedback agreement must be transparent.

Care, Growth Operations, template licensing, and niche SEO retainers are deferred. A real client request can justify a separately scoped support arrangement; a speculative product menu cannot.

Before testing this price, use one comparable benchmark proposal:

- up to eight authored marketing routes;
- one CMS collection with setup and initial population of up to 12 records;
- two initial creative directions, one selected system, and two consolidated revision rounds after direction selection;
- client-supplied and client-approved business facts, reviews, credentials, service details, and source media;
- Treez-led hierarchy, core-page writing and editing from those materials, with extensive interviewing or long-form content separately scoped;
- one original signature media treatment, with any third-party licensing or generation budget approved separately;
- one primary inquiry form, service-intent carryover, agreed analytics events, and up to 25 redirects;
- launch support and a 30-day defect period; and
- no ongoing content, maintenance, search, advertising, or unlimited support.

If the actual project differs materially, change the price and record the scope difference. Before the first proposal, estimate delivery hours, value Treez’s labor, identify direct external costs, and record the minimum acceptable contribution result. This is a cost floor, not a public hourly rate.

## Roofing-first portfolio model

### Entry 1: Ascension Roofing

Ascension remains client work and is never counted as fictional inventory.

Before public use:

- obtain Joshua’s written permission for the exact material shown;
- state “active rebuild” and “production results are not yet available” near the title;
- do not expose the client’s poor legacy traffic, internal weaknesses, private facts, or unapproved comparisons;
- distinguish completed work from planned or client-dependent work; and
- remove or generalize sensitive project and business information.

The initial capability case may show:

- the original site problem and content complexity without ridicule;
- why residential roofing became the primary positioning;
- the selected brand direction and original asset system;
- the Roof Record concept and client-facing CMS workflow;
- service-intent carryover into the contact experience;
- responsive behavior, premium motion, accessibility, and reduced-motion behavior;
- performance, image-delivery, form, 404, and QA evidence; and
- what remains before production launch.

Before launch, capture and date the available legacy Search Console, inquiry, and qualified-lead facts. If tracking or volume is unusable, record that limitation rather than inventing a baseline. After launch, begin the comparison period. With low volume, publish absolute facts and limitations rather than dramatic percentage claims. Outcome claims require the separate gate below.

### Entry 2: fictional roofing concept 01

Build one concept only after a light research brief identifies a genuinely different roofing business hypothesis. Examples of a meaningful difference include commercial versus residential focus, restoration versus architectural metal specialization, or a different buyer-risk and proof model. The concept may not reuse Ascension’s brand world, Weather Shield, Roof Record expression, palette, copy, composition, or assets.

The smallest persuasive artifact is a polished responsive vertical slice:

1. opening proposition and signature visual idea;
2. one representative service and proof passage;
3. a clear custom-engagement invitation; and
4. enough mobile and interaction behavior to demonstrate the intended craft.

It is not automatically a complete business website. Additional routes are permitted only when they prove a material capability requested by a prospect or needed by the concept.

Required disclosure:

> Fictional roofing design concept. This demonstrates design and implementation capability, not client work or business results.

If an interactive preview exists, it must:

- use `noindex, nofollow`, remain outside XML sitemaps, and stay crawlable so the directive can be processed;
- avoid LocalBusiness, Review, AggregateRating, fake maps, fabricated service areas, testimonials, credentials, awards, warranties, results, and availability;
- use safe example contact values or non-submitting forms; and
- retain essential meaning on mobile, keyboard, reduced motion, slow media, and JavaScript failure.

The indexed Work entry should contain original design analysis and the disclosure. It should sell a new custom engagement at this level of craft, never “this template,” “this edition,” or a reskin.

## Acquisition experiment

The marketing site is the destination, not the acquisition strategy. The first sales loop should stay deliberately manual so Treez learns the language and objections before automating anything.

### Prospect fit

Prioritize roofing companies with:

- real reviews, projects, service facts, and visible operating credibility;
- a website problem that can be shown without exaggeration;
- valuable jobs and capacity for additional good-fit work;
- a reachable owner or decision-maker; and
- evidence that demand already exists through referrals, profile activity, advertising, or search.

Avoid businesses expecting a website to rescue an unproven operation, missing fulfillment capacity, or unwilling to provide real proof.

### Six-week manual test

1. Build an initial list of 30 carefully matched roofing companies.
2. Each week, create up to five short, genuinely personalized website observations or two-minute walkthroughs for the best matches.
3. Follow the observation with a concise call or email and one useful follow-up. Stop politely when interest is absent.
4. Offer a simple founder-completed website review or 15-minute project conversation, not a free speculative redesign.
5. Publish short process walkthroughs from Build With Treez or permissioned Ascension material so prospects can see how Treez thinks and communicates.
6. Ask existing clients and trusted local partners for introductions. Photographers, sign shops, ad managers, and Business Profile specialists can be useful complementary relationships.
7. Record the source, observable problem, reply, conversation quality, objection, proposal, and decision in a simple sheet. Do not build a CRM or add an AIEOS workflow yet.

Lead with one observable business or trust problem, not “I build beautiful websites.” The conversation should determine whether the real constraint is the site, offer clarity, proof, follow-up, reviews, profile quality, or operating capacity. Say so honestly when a website is not the first problem.

## Validation and expansion gates

These gates exist to prevent speculative production. They are learning thresholds, not credentials Treez must earn before speaking to a client.

### Gate A: publish the initial portfolio

- Build With Treez clearly states the founder, offer, audience, boundaries, and next step.
- Treez has approved the new Build With Treez design contract and the rendered marketing-site and concept vertical slices.
- Ascension is public only in the form Joshua authorizes and carries the active-rebuild disclosure.
- Fictional work is unmistakably labeled and contains no fabricated business evidence.
- The marketing site and concept pass mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, slow-media, no-JavaScript, form, broken-route, and the specific performance-budget checks recorded in the future design or launch contract.
- Public claims match documented evidence.

Passing Gate A authorizes active selling. It does not require traffic or client-outcome results.

### Gate B: build a second fictional roofing concept

Authorize concept 02 only when all are true:

- at least 10 matched roofing decision-makers have actually seen the current work or walkthrough;
- at least three provide substantive reactions, objections, or questions; and
- at least two independent decision-makers identify the same materially different customer or proof need that the current work cannot demonstrate, recorded in their own language.

If the gap is messaging, trust, or sales presentation, improve the existing site instead of producing another concept.

### Gate C: expand to a second niche

Do not begin plumbing, lawn care, or another niche concept until:

- at least one roofing engagement has a paid deposit and a recorded source reported by the prospect or captured by a campaign or referral path;
- that engagement reaches client-approved launch readiness or completion without an unresolved pattern of scope, approval, accessibility, performance, or support failure;
- contracted and collected revenue, valued delivery labor, and direct external costs have been reviewed against the contribution floor recorded before the proposal, or Treez explicitly records why he is accepting an exception;
- at least one client-authorized capability case exists; and
- the next niche is chosen from evidence: either three qualified conversations reveal repeated demand in that trade or one strong-fit paid opportunity already exists.

Plumbing is a candidate, not a preset decision.

### Gate D: make commercial outcome claims

An outcome claim requires:

- a defined pre-launch baseline and measurement period;
- at least 90 days of appropriate post-launch field data unless seasonality or low volume requires longer;
- owned form and call tracking or another reliable inquiry record;
- client confirmation of lead quality where relevant;
- exact dates, sample size, limitations, and source; and
- the client’s permission to publish.

Lab performance and accessibility checks may prove implementation quality. They do not prove traffic, leads, revenue, or ROI.

## Funnel diagnosis

- Fewer than three engaged replies after 20 well-matched contacts: revise targeting and the opening observation before building more work.
- Replies but no qualified conversations: revise the CTA, trust signals, or fit criteria.
- Conversations but no proposals: inspect whether the actual need, budget, urgency, and controlled promise align.
- Three serious proposals without a win: reassess proof, scope, risk, price, and the decision process with direct prospect feedback.
- A paid project with weak delivery economics: correct scope and workflow before increasing volume.
- No paid engagement after the full first test: stop producing concepts and conduct owner interviews. Do not answer the failure with another niche library.

## Delivery workflow

### Real client work

Use the premium-client-site stages:

1. business diagnosis;
2. demand and conversion map;
3. exactly two creative directions;
4. human direction selection;
5. locked design contract;
6. one vertical slice;
7. human creative approval;
8. site expansion;
9. independent creative, commercial, and technical reviews; and
10. launch scorecard and learning review.

### Fictional concept work

Use only the proportionate subset:

1. research brief and evidence boundaries;
2. distinct customer and creative thesis;
3. two direction studies and Treez’s selection;
4. one responsive vertical slice;
5. creative and technical review; and
6. clear publication disclosure.

There is no client launch governance, CMS, full route expansion, or 30/60/90 review unless the concept is later commissioned as a new custom engagement.

AIEOS is added only when a project crosses into meaningful integration, data, security, cost, or coordination risk. It does not replace the creative contract or create a second approval ledger.

## IP and originality

Reusable assets are limited to pre-existing tools, workflows, generic accessibility and form primitives, CMS field patterns, image delivery, analytics vocabulary, QA scripts, launch scorecards, and invisible layout utilities.

Never reuse without explicit authorization:

- client marks, names, copy, photographs, reviews, project data, or customer language;
- client-specific illustrations, animations, proof-system names, or signature visual treatment;
- market claims, locations, structured data, or case-study outcomes; or
- an approved client composition with only colors and logos changed.

Each concept requires a distinct customer hypothesis, creative thesis, three physical voice words, signature visual idea, proof mechanism, palette, typography posture, layout logic, and motion posture.

AI-assisted media requires an asset manifest and meaningful human art direction, selection, arrangement, and editing. Do not promise that a raw generated output is exclusively copyrightable. Contract language should be reviewed by qualified counsel before becoming standard.

## Stop conditions

Stop and request Treez’s decision when:

- public copy exceeds the evidence baseline;
- a concept could be mistaken for client work or a real business;
- Ascension display lacks Joshua’s permission;
- a client-specific expression would be reused;
- a second concept or niche lacks its required gate evidence;
- a recurring growth promise cannot be measured or controlled;
- a framework, CMS, animation package, model provider, automation, or AIEOS expansion lacks a project need;
- a material creative direction lacks human approval; or
- a legal, licensing, accessibility, privacy, security, or advertising concern remains unresolved.
