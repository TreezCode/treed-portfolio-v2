# Build With Treez Industry Concepts Plan

## Authority

- Status: **LOCKED**
- Version: **1.0**
- Decision owner: Treez
- Recorded: August 3, 2026
- Applies to: the future `buildwithtreez.com` web-services marketing site, its concept library, and any niche demonstrations created to support sales
- Authority relationship: `PRODUCT.md` is the canonical business authority. This file is its detailed execution, validation, IP, and change-control plan. If the two conflict, `PRODUCT.md` wins and this plan must be corrected.

## Strategic decision

Build With Treez will become a focused marketing website for premium web services. **Industry Concepts** will be a proof and sales system inside that site, not a generic template marketplace.

The model has two parts:

1. An indexable agency library explains each niche concept, the business problem it addresses, the design reasoning, and the conversion journey.
2. Clearly fictional, noindexed interactive demonstrations show the quality of the work and lead to a consultation for a custom client edition.

Revenue comes from productized custom engagements and optional ongoing services. The concepts demonstrate capability; they are not the primary product.

## Locked decisions

1. **Sell custom outcomes, not downloadable templates.** Reuse the process and invisible infrastructure while giving each client an original brand thesis, imagery, copy, proof, and market strategy.
2. **Build one adjacent concept first.** Plumbing is the first fictional concept after Ascension. Lawn care and every later niche remain blocked until the plumbing experiment passes its validation gate.
3. **Protect Ascension.** Ascension's visual direction, Weather Shield, Roof Record expression, copy, project evidence, and branded execution will never be resold as a roofing template. Portfolio display requires Joshua's permission.
4. **Index explanation, not fictional local businesses.** Agency concept articles may be indexed. Full fictional demonstrations are labeled and `noindex`; they do not use fake reviews, addresses, maps, results, service areas, or LocalBusiness schema.
5. **Award-caliber is a production standard, not a marketing claim.** Build With Treez may describe its process and quality precisely. It may not claim award-winning work until a real award exists.
6. **Traffic is a separate operating promise.** A premium site is the conversion foundation. Search, Business Profile work, content, outreach, and campaign operations are separate services with separate scope and measurement.
7. **AIEOS remains proportional.** The premium-client-site skill owns the marketing-site workflow. AIEOS is used only when integrations, data, security, cost, or team coordination justify its governance. This strategy does not authorize expanding the AIEOS kernel.

## Business model

### Core offer: Premium Custom Edition

A fixed, productized engagement that still produces a client-specific result.

Required characteristics:

- business diagnosis and demand mapping;
- two materially different creative directions;
- one human-approved direction before full expansion;
- original visual thesis and signature asset system;
- conversion copy and credible proof architecture;
- responsive, accessible, performant implementation;
- qualified contact path and measurement contract;
- launch scorecard and 30, 60, and 90 day review plan.

Founding price hypothesis: **$8,000 to $12,000** for tightly scoped early engagements. After two or three verified commercial case studies, test **$12,000 to $20,000 or more** based on scope, content production, CMS needs, and integrations. These are experiment ranges, not published promises.

### Optional offer: Care and Measurement

Suggested hypothesis: **$300 to $750 per month**, depending on hosting oversight, dependency maintenance, content assistance, reporting, form monitoring, and response expectations.

This is not an unlimited-change subscription. Define response windows, included work, ownership, and overage terms.

### Separate offer: Growth Operations

Potential services include local search support, Google Business Profile work, first-hand content, project publishing, landing-page testing, and conversion reporting. Suggested early hypothesis: **$1,500 to $3,000 or more per month** only after delivery capability and measurement ownership are proven.

Do not sell vague SEO retainers or promise traffic, rankings, or lead volume without evidence and controllable scope.

### Deferred offer: Fast-Track Edition

A partially prebuilt, limited-license edition may be tested later if custom engagements reveal repeatable demand. It must be sold honestly as a faster edition, not bespoke work.

Any exclusivity must define:

- vertical;
- geography;
- term and renewal;
- what visual and technical elements are exclusive;
- buyout or termination terms;
- the distinction between contractual exclusivity and copyright ownership.

This offer remains deferred until the custom model has verified demand and margins.

## Target customer and buying trigger

Primary customer: an owner-led local-service company with a credible operation, sufficient project value, and weak digital presentation that needs a website capable of supporting trust, qualified inquiries, and future marketing.

Useful signals:

- average project value can support an $8,000 or greater website investment;
- the owner can provide real work, customer language, and operational facts;
- the company has an active Business Profile, referrals, outbound activity, or existing traffic worth converting;
- the current site is generic, outdated, insecure, slow, unclear, or difficult to maintain;
- the owner values differentiation and measurement rather than requesting only a cheaper brochure site.

Primary buying triggers:

- an outdated or damaged website is losing trust;
- a new service, territory, or growth phase requires clearer positioning;
- paid or organic traffic reaches a site that does not convert;
- competitors present stronger proof;
- the owner needs a credible sales asset before increasing outreach or marketing spend.

## Industry Concepts presentation model

### Indexed concept article

Each article should include:

- the niche and profitable customer type;
- common buying triggers and trust failures;
- the proposed offer and primary conversion;
- the concept's original creative thesis;
- its proof mechanism and why it belongs to that trade;
- representative desktop and mobile views;
- a transparent fictional-concept disclosure;
- a direct invitation to commission a custom edition.

The article must contain original analysis. It cannot be a thin wrapper around screenshots.

### Full interactive demonstration

Minimum buyer journey:

1. Home or opening proposition
2. One representative service page
3. One proof or case-record experience
4. Intent-aware contact path

The demo may include additional pages only when they prove a material capability. Ten speculative pages are not more persuasive than one complete journey.

Every demo must:

- use fictional, non-geographic business details;
- display `Concept project, not a client result` prominently;
- use `noindex, nofollow` and remain outside XML sitemaps;
- remain crawlable so search engines can process `noindex`; do not block the demo in `robots.txt`;
- send an `X-Robots-Tag` noindex header where the selected hosting architecture supports it;
- avoid LocalBusiness, Review, AggregateRating, or fabricated outcome schema;
- use safe example phone and email values or non-submitting forms;
- avoid fake testimonials, rankings, warranties, certifications, availability, or performance claims;
- provide complete mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, slow-media, and JavaScript-failure behavior.

## Concept originality standard

Repeat the workflow, not the result.

Reusable across engagements:

- repository and deployment patterns;
- accessibility primitives;
- form validation and delivery boundaries;
- CMS field patterns;
- image delivery;
- analytics event vocabulary;
- QA scripts and launch scorecards;
- generic layout and interaction utilities that are invisible to the buyer.

Never reuse without explicit authorization:

- client marks, names, copy, photographs, reviews, project data, or customer language;
- client-specific signature illustrations or animations;
- a proof-system name or visual expression that has become part of a client's identity;
- market claims, local pages, structured data, or case-study outcomes;
- an approved client composition with only colors and logos changed.

Each concept requires:

- one trade-rooted creative thesis;
- three physical voice words;
- one signature visual idea;
- one proof mechanism that fits the business operation;
- a distinct palette, typography posture, layout logic, and motion posture;
- two direction studies and Treez's explicit selection;
- creative, commercial, and technical reviews.

## First experiment: plumbing

Plumbing is the only approved concept to begin after the Build With Treez strategy and offer are ready.

It must not inherit Ascension's High-Desert Roofcraft world, Weather Shield, seam language, rust and teal palette, or Roof Record. Its proof mechanism should emerge from plumbing operations and customer risk. Possible territories to investigate include diagnosis trace, service ledger, water-path evidence, repair verification, and property protection. None is approved until research and direction studies are complete.

The plumbing concept stops after one vertical slice until Treez approves the rendered direction.

## Distribution plan

The concept library does not generate demand by existing. Distribution is a separate operating system.

Initial channels:

- personalized outreach to carefully matched businesses;
- two to four minute video audits that point to a relevant concept decision;
- short process videos showing strategy, art direction, proof design, and measurement;
- referrals and direct introductions;
- client-authorized case studies;
- focused search content on the agency site.

Do not add CRM automation, content calendars, or outbound machinery to the premium-client-site skill or AIEOS kernel. Manage those as a separate sales and audience workflow.

## Ninety-day validation plan

### Days 1 to 14: offer and foundation

- Complete the Build With Treez business diagnosis and current-site audit.
- Confirm the primary audience, flagship offer, qualification criteria, and contact action.
- Define scope exclusions, pricing hypotheses, ownership, portfolio rights, and exclusivity language.
- Confirm the proposed site architecture and Industry Concepts presentation model.
- Request Joshua's permission before treating Ascension as public portfolio work.

### Days 15 to 42: one concept

- Research plumbing buyers, service economics, common objections, competitors, search intent, and proof practices.
- Produce exactly two visual directions through the premium-client-site workflow.
- Select one direction through Treez's human creative gate.
- Build only the approved buyer journey: opening, one service, one proof experience, and contact.
- Record hours, direct costs, revisions, model usage, and reusable versus unique work.

### Days 43 to 56: publish the sales surface

- Publish the Build With Treez Industry Concepts hub and plumbing article.
- Publish the full plumbing demonstration as clearly fictional and `noindex`.
- Add concept-view, demo-view, qualified-contact, and source measurement.
- Publish Ascension only in the status and form Joshua authorizes.

### Days 57 to 84: direct validation

- Build a list of 100 to 150 genuinely matched plumbing businesses.
- Send personalized outreach and short walkthrough videos.
- Publish 8 to 12 useful process or marketing videos.
- Record reply, qualified call, proposal, close, delivery-hour, and source data.

### Days 85 to 90: decision

- Review offer fit, proof, close rate, delivery time, direct cost, and expected margin.
- Correct targeting when replies and calls are weak.
- Correct proof, positioning, or sales when calls occur but proposals do not close.
- Correct scope or workflow when projects close but margins are weak.
- Approve, revise, or stop the model before authorizing another niche.

## Validation gates

Do not build niche concept number three until plumbing produces:

- at least 100 matched prospects contacted;
- at least 5 qualified calls or 1 paid project at $8,000 or more;
- at least 2 proposals when enough qualified calls exist;
- estimated delivery of 80 hours or less for the agreed founding scope;
- target gross margin of at least 60 percent;
- no unresolved pattern of accessibility, performance, client-approval, or support failures.

If the commercial gate fails, improve or stop the offer. Do not respond by producing more speculative concepts.

## Measurement contract

Primary measures:

- qualified fit-call requests;
- qualified calls by concept and source;
- proposals issued;
- engagements closed;
- attributable project value;
- delivery hours and direct production cost;
- gross margin;
- care or growth attachment rate.

Diagnostic measures:

- concept article views;
- full-demo opens;
- article-to-demo rate;
- demo-to-contact rate;
- outreach reply rate;
- form abandonment;
- organic queries and non-branded visibility.

Traffic, impressions, awards, and social reach are not business outcomes by themselves.

## IP and client protection

Future agreements should distinguish:

- Build With Treez pre-existing tools, workflows, generic components, schemas, and reusable know-how;
- client-owned logos, copy, photographs, project data, testimonials, and business records;
- ownership or exclusive license for paid, client-specific branded expression;
- portfolio-display permission;
- third-party asset, font, model, and media licenses;
- geography and term for any Fast-Track exclusivity.

AI-assisted media requires an asset manifest and meaningful human art direction, selection, arrangement, and editing. Do not promise that a raw generated output is exclusively copyrightable.

Contract language should be reviewed by qualified counsel before becoming a standard client agreement.

## Delivery workflow

Every real client website and concept follows the premium-client-site stages:

1. Business diagnosis
2. Demand and conversion map
3. Exactly two creative directions
4. Human direction selection
5. Locked design contract
6. One vertical slice
7. Human creative approval
8. Site expansion
9. Independent creative, commercial, and technical reviews
10. Launch scorecard and learning review

AIEOS is added only when a project crosses into meaningful integration, data, security, cost, or coordination risk. It does not replace the creative contract or create a second approval ledger.

## Stop conditions

Stop and request a Treez decision when:

- the Build With Treez buyer, offer, primary conversion, or price position is unresolved;
- a concept could be confused with a real company or client result;
- a niche cannot support original proof or operational specificity;
- a client-specific expression would be reused;
- a third niche is proposed before the plumbing validation gate;
- a growth promise cannot be measured or controlled;
- a tool, framework, CMS, animation package, model provider, or AIEOS expansion lacks a project need;
- a material creative direction has not received human approval;
- a legal, licensing, accessibility, privacy, security, or advertising concern remains unresolved.
